document name: Information and Security Acceptable Use Policy

Document ID: AUP 001

Effective Date: September 2026

Policy Owner: Information Security Compliance Team

Purpose: The purpose of this policy is to protect corperate assets aginst unauthorized access, data leaks, and ransomware.

Scope: This policy applies to all employees and personel with login access to company data or company network.

Drive Encryption: All laptops and Personal computers must have full disk encryption enabled using BitLocker.

Screen Locks: All unnattended devices must automaticly lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.

Multi-Factor Authentication: MANDATORY FOR ALL PERSONEL. using a FID02 key.

Password Strength: Passwords or passphrases must be a minimum of 14 characters long.

We have three new activities that are explicitly forbidden on company networks: 
1: Connecting unauthorized personal usb drives or disks. 
2: Storing company data on personal cloud drives such as personal DropBox.    
3: Disabling or uninstalling local security software.

please respect these new changes and comform to them immediately. thank you for keeping you and the company safe.
