# Corporate Information Security Policy
**Document ID:** POL-SEC-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** August 2026
**Owner:** Information Security & Compliance Team

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
* **Purpose:** This policy defines mandatory security standards to protect company systems, endpoint devices, and data assets against unauthorized access, malware infections, and data exposure.
* **Scope:** Applies to all full-time employees, contractors, and third-party partners accessing corporate infrastructure or cloud environments.

## 2. Hardware & Device Security
* **Drive Encryption:** All corporate laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled using BitLocker (Windows) or FileVault (macOS).
* **Screen Locks:** Unattended devices must automatically lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Users must manually lock screens (`Win + L`) when stepping away.

## 3. Identity & Access Standards
* **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Mandatory for all accounts using hardware security keys (FIDO2) or authenticator applications (TOTP). SMS-based MFA is prohibited.
* **Password Length:** Passwords and passphrases must contain a minimum of 14 characters and undergo automated checking against breached password lists.

## 4. Prohibited Activities
1. Connecting unauthorized USB drives or personal storage media to corporate endpoints.
2. Storing company sensitive data on unapproved personal cloud accounts (e.g., personal Google Drive or Dropbox).
3. Disabling, altering, or uninstalling local endpoint detection and response (EDR) software.
